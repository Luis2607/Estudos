# Mission: English for International Design Work

## Why
Become operationally fluent in English to sell, negotiate, and operate a premium international design and product studio. English is the main bottleneck between Luis's current work and the international projects he wants to win.

## Success looks like
- Run sales, discovery, and project calls in English without freezing.
- Explain the value of landing pages, product redesigns, UX decisions, and retainer offers clearly.
- Negotiate price, scope, deadlines, revisions, and payment terms professionally.
- Write agency website copy, proposals, case studies, and outreach messages in natural English.
- Consume UX, product, startup, and music content with increasing comfort.
- Think partially in English during real work situations instead of translating every sentence.

## Constraints
- Lessons should be short, practical, visual when useful, and tied to real work scenarios.
- Use guided learning: context, attempt, feedback, refinement, and repetition.
- Correct by layers: meaning, grammar or concept, naturalness, and professional tone.
- Complement private English classes with daily deliberate practice, roleplay, feedback loops, and review.
- Avoid long abstract grammar lectures unless the grammar is needed for a real task.

## Out of scope
- School-style grammar drills disconnected from real use.
- Accent perfection or trying to sound native at all costs.
- Random vocabulary lists disconnected from work, music, business, or life.
