# English Learning Notes

- Luis learns best through guided, adaptive, nonlinear learning: context → attempt → feedback → refinement → transfer.
- Ask Luis to try before giving a complete answer.
- Use micro-steps and avoid long walls of theory.
- Correct by layers: meaning → grammar/concept → naturalness → professional tone.
- Use real scenarios from his life: international clients, landing pages, CalcMed, UX critique, proposals, pricing, scope, deadlines, agency positioning, and subscription design services.
- Use Portuguese for deep explanation when needed, but keep practice and key chunks in English.
- Priorities: work in English, consume English content, think in English, and understand music.
- Music interests: The Beatles, Arctic Monkeys, Oasis, especially challenging lyrics such as “Tranquility Base Hotel & Casino”.
- Luis wants daily deliberate practice and an intensive but sustainable immersion system.
- Avoid asking him to choose the lesson every time. Use the curriculum and learning records to guide the next step.
- Audio/walking sessions should be especially clear, short, and teacher-led.
- Do not move on merely because something was explained. Look for independent transfer to a new scenario.
