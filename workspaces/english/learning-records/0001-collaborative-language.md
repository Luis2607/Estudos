# Learning Record 0001

## Topic
Collaborative and professional English.

## What was learned
Luis naturally uses collaborative language and softeners in professional English discussions.

Examples:
- Maybe we could...
- What if we...
- I think...
- It could...
- I'll be upfront with you...

## Real applications
- UX critique
- Client negotiation
- Scope discussion
- Deadline negotiation
- Product and design reviews

## Important insight
Professional English is not only grammar. Tone, emotional energy, and collaboration matter a lot in international work environments.
